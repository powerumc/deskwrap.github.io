DeskWrap Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Rivon Labs (“we” or “us”) handles personal information in operating DeskWrap (the “Software”) and this website. It covers what Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and Article 13 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) require us to tell you. This policy is provided in Korean and English; if the two versions differ, the Korean version prevails.

In short: the Software connects your Macs directly to each other. App window images, keyboard and mouse input, sound, and what you copy travel only between your own Macs and never pass through a server of ours (we don’t run one). The Software has no accounts, no ads, and collects no usage analytics. This website uses Google Analytics for visitor statistics (see 4 below). Apart from that, the only personal information we receive is the emails you send us.

1. Information that stays on your Macs

The following is processed only on your Mac, or between the Macs you connect, and is never sent to us. We cannot receive or see it. Below, the “host Mac” is the Mac where an app runs, and “this Mac” is the Mac where you use that app.

  • App window images and sound: the windows of the apps you pick, and those apps’ sound, are sent from the host Mac to this Mac. Only the windows of the apps you pick are sent, never the whole screen, and the images and sound received are not saved.
  • Keyboard and mouse input: keys you press and mouse actions in a mirrored window are passed to that app on the host Mac. The text you type is not recorded.
  • Clipboard: when you copy on one Mac, the other Mac is told only that something was copied and in what formats; the content (text or images) crosses over when you paste. However, if the last thing you copied came from a Mac when you close the connection to it, its content is fetched in advance so you can still paste it after the connection closes.
  • Mac identity and connection information: each Mac creates an identity (a cryptographic key) used to verify connections and keeps it in that Mac’s keychain. The list of Macs you have connected to (name, address, and identity fingerprint) and the list of devices you have allowed to connect are kept in files that only your user account can read.
  • Proxy apps: to show remote apps in the Dock and ⌘Tab, the Software creates a small app with each remote app’s name and icon in the ~/Applications/DeskWrap folder on this Mac.
  • Announcing to nearby Macs: a Mac turned on as a host announces its Mac name, the first 8 characters of its identity fingerprint, its protocol version, and whether it is currently accepting a pairing code on the local network (Bonjour), so that Macs on the same network can find it. Other devices on the same network can also see this announcement. Nothing is announced when the host is turned off.
  • Settings: the Software’s settings, such as whether to check for updates automatically.
  • Logs: troubleshooting logs are kept in the ~/Library/Logs/DeskWrap folder on this Mac. They record the course of connections and errors, the names, addresses, and identity fingerprints of the Macs you connect to, and the bundle IDs of mirrored apps; they do not record screen images, the text you type, or what you copy. Only your user account can read the files (permission 0600), and once a file passes 8 MB, only the previous file is kept and a new one is started.
  • Diagnostic files: “Problem Report” creates a file that you save yourself. Before logs are added to it, Mac names, addresses, pairing codes, and home folder paths are masked, and identity fingerprints are cut to their first 8 characters. The Software never sends this file anywhere. We receive it only if you attach it to an email (see 2).

4. This website

  • Visitor statistics (Google Analytics): To improve the website, we use Google Analytics, a service provided by Google LLC, to see visitor statistics. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information about how you use the website, such as the pages you visit, how you arrived, your device and browser, and your approximate location estimated from your IP address. It does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address, and we do not combine this information with other information or use it for advertising. The information is processed on Google’s servers, which may be located outside your country, including in the United States, and individual usage records are deleted after two months. The cookies stay in your browser for up to two years after your last visit. To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • How to opt out: Block cookies in your browser settings or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, and nothing is collected. Nothing is sent if you turn on Do Not Track in your browser either. Opting out does not limit your use of the website.
  • Visitors in the EU, EEA, and UK: The legal basis for this processing is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in improving the website), and you can object at any time using the methods above (Article 21). Safeguards for international transfers are in 6 below.
  • Apart from Google Analytics, there are no tracking tools or scripts or fonts loaded from elsewhere. This website serves its fonts itself.
  • To remember your language choice (Korean or English) for your next visit, we store it only in your browser’s local storage. It is not sent to us, and it is removed when you clear the site’s data in your browser.
  • This website is served by GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process access records (IP address, time, browser information); we do not receive them.
  • Cloudflare resolves our domain name (DNS). Your connection goes directly to GitHub and does not pass through Cloudflare.

7. Deletion

When a retention period ends or the purpose is achieved, we delete the information without delay. Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered, and emails are removed from the trash as well.

8. Your rights

  • You may ask us to give you access to, correct, delete, or stop processing your personal information (PIPA Arts. 35–37). If you are in the EU, EEA, or UK, you also have the GDPR rights of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection (Art. 21).
  • Email contact@rivonlabs.com. A representative (a legal guardian or a person you authorize) may also make the request. We will reply within 10 days after confirming your identity (within one month where the GDPR applies).
  • We do not hold the information on your Macs (section 1), so you delete it yourself: unpair allowed devices in Settings › Host on the host Mac, and delete the ~/Library/Logs/DeskWrap folder to remove the logs. When you delete the Software, you can remove the rest of this information as well.
  • You may file a complaint with the Personal Information Protection Commission of Korea. If you are in the EU, EEA, or UK, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country (GDPR Art. 77).

9. Security measures

  • The Software: Mac-to-Mac connections are encrypted with TLS 1.3 and made only with Macs verified with a pairing code. Identities are kept in the keychain, and connection lists and logs are kept in files that only your user account can open. Updates and compatibility settings are used only after their signatures are verified. Internet connections use HTTPS.
  • Rivon Labs: we run no servers that hold personal information. Our email account is used only by the person in charge and is protected with two-step verification.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.
